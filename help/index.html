TSE portfolio · how to keep it current

What do you want to do today?

Open the portfolio folder in Claude, find your situation below, and say the sentence. Claude edits the page and the fact sheet behind it, checks the numbers, and nothing goes public until you say "publish". Every sentence here has a copy button.

STEP 1
Open the folder in Claude

Cowork: add the portfolio folder. Terminal: cd portfolio && claude. The skills load by themselves.

STEP 2
Say what changed

In plain words, with names, dates and numbers. Claude asks if something is missing; it never guesses a figure.

STEP 3
Read the summary

Claude lists the edits and the result of the consistency check.

STEP 4
Say "publish"

Checks, commit, push. Live at your domain about a minute later.

Three sources of numbers

headline

Scopus and Google Scholar

Your career totals: 380+ publications, h-index 53, 10k+ citations (Scopus, Sept 2026). You read them from your profile and tell Claude. These are the numbers you quote.

charts

OpenAlex

Free and scriptable. Drives works-per-year, most-cited papers and partner institutions. Counts preprints and reports too, so its totals run above Scopus; the site says which is which.

yours

Your own records

Team, alumni, projects, funding. Only you can keep these right, and they are what visitors notice first.

If something looks off

Claude says the consistency check failed

It found two numbers that disagree, for example 24 people listed but a count of 23. Tell Claude which is right; it fixes the other. Never ask it to "make the check pass" without saying which number is correct.

The live site did not change after publishing

Wait two minutes and reload with Shift held. If still old: "Check whether the deploy went through." Claude looks at git and GitHub Pages and reports.

The page is blank or says it cannot load data

You opened index.html from your disk. Ask Claude for a local preview, or look at the live site.

Claude asks me for a number I don't have

Say "leave it out" and the field stays empty. The site never shows a guessed figure.

I want to change how the site looks

Describe it: "Make the hero numbers bigger", "add a photo of the team on the people section". That edits index.html and assets/; Claude will preview before publishing.
