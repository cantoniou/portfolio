/ 01 — Profile

Professor of transportation systems engineering at the Technical University of Munich.

Constantinos Antoniou heads the Chair of Transportation Systems Engineering at TUM. His work concerns the modelling, simulation and calibration of transportation systems — from dynamic traffic assignment to intelligent transportation systems and road safety.

He studied civil engineering at NTUA and earned his MS and PhD at MIT, then held faculty posts at NTUA before joining TUM in 2016.
