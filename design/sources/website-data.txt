Prof. Dr. Constantinos Antoniou has been a Full Professor and head of the Chair of Transportation Systems Engineering at TUM since 1 September 2016. He was born in 1973. He earned an NTUA Diploma in Civil Engineering (1995), then an MIT MS in Transportation (1997) and an MIT PhD in Transportation Systems (2004). He was a postdoc at MIT (2004 to 2008) and a faculty member at NTUA (2009 to 2015). He held visiting posts at EPFL and ETH Zurich in 2016. He holds a 2023 Francqui Chair at UHasselt and was named a Humboldt Foundation Henriette Herz Scout in December 2025.

Education
NTUA (1990 to 1995). He earned a 5-year Diploma in Civil Engineering with a Transportation specialisation. His diploma thesis was “Development of a Geographical Information System for the management of road safety” (in Greek). He submitted it in June 1995 to the Department of Transportation Planning and Engineering.
MIT MS in Transportation (1997). His thesis was supervised by Prof. Moshe Ben-Akiva. He was a full-time research assistant in MIT’s Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering from 1995 to 1997. The thesis won the 1997 Milton Pikarsky Award “for an outstanding Master of Science thesis” from the Council of University Transportation Centers.
MIT PhD in Transportation Systems (2004). Thesis titled “On-line calibration for dynamic traffic assignment” in 2004 at the Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering. The advisors were Moshe E. Ben-Akiva and Haris N. Koutsopoulos.
Honours during and just after his studies:
MIT full-time research assistantships, 1995 to 1997 and 2001 to 2004
An FHWA travel grant for IEEE ITSC 2004 in Washington, D.C.
Early career (1997 to 2009)
DENCO Ltd., Athens (1997). Transportation Engineer. He ran a traffic simulation for the planned Malliakos Gulf underwater tunnel.
IBI Group UK Ltd., Athens (1999 to 2000). Senior Transportation Engineer. He worked on the Integrated Toll and Traffic Management System of the Attiki Odos tollway.
TRD International SA, Athens (2000 to 2001). Senior Transportation Consultant. He developed software, including components of the container terminal management system at the Port of Thessaloniki.
NTUA (April 2000 to March 2008). Research Associate on EU road safety and ITS projects, including ADVISORS and SafetyNet.
MIT (October 2004 to June 2008). Post-doctoral Research Associate, including technical lead roles on research projects.
TREDIT S.A., Athens (June 2006 to June 2008). Director of the Athens branch and PI of EU projects including FREIGHTWISE, EFFORTS, EURIDICE and PROMIT.
MIT (from July 2008). Research Affiliate, including work with the Singapore-MIT Alliance for Research and Technology (SMART).
He also held a Marie Curie International Reintegration Grant (2006 to 2008, project FLEXIBLETRAFFIC). In 2005 to 2006 he received an “All-round excellence” grant from Austrian Research Centers (Arsenal Research, Vienna).
