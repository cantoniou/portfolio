How we work

Four research pillars. One toolbox.

MODELING & SIMULATION 01

Cities in silico

Micro and mesoscopic simulation · SUMO, VISUM, MATSim, SimMobility, Aimsun · graph neural networks and transfer learning

CALIBRATION & OPTIMIZATION 02

Making large models trustworthy

Offline and online calibration of noisy, large-scale models · W-SPSA, PCA-GLS, PCA-EKF, PC-SPSA

ROAD SAFETY & HUMAN FACTORS 03

How people drive and decide

Driving simulator and eye tracking · one of the largest naturalistic driving datasets, via i-DREAMS · acceptance of new modes

BIG DATA & MACHINE LEARNING 04

Learning from traffic data

Data-driven models of driving behaviour and traffic dynamics · interpretable, transferable prediction

Underneath all four: statistics and machine learning, discrete choice, simulation, optimisation. 65+ research topics, 90+ journals.

Milestones17

The next decade

Four directions, already funded through 2029.

01 → 2029

AI-driven transport engineering

Geometric deep learning that transfers between cities and learns from live data: traffic management, resilience, demand prediction.

02 → 2029

Mobility behaviour modelling

How people adapt under extreme events; networks that anticipate and recover from disruption.

03 → 2029

Autonomous & connected mobility

Mixed traffic, connected infrastructure, safety first. Our own smartphone app, from VOTAVI, to measure behavioural response to AVs.

04 → 2029

Climate & sustainable transport

Data-driven emission models for cities and freight; zero-shot transfer to estimate traffic and emissions where data is scarce.

Carried by i-TRAUM, AgiMo, PROMETHEUS, SEE-FAr, SCUDO, MI-TRAP, VOTAVI and AUTOSUP.
